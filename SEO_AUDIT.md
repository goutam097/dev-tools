## Search Console opportunity triage

No page/query export is present, so exact P0 URLs by impressions and position cannot be established. Use Search Console’s page and query export to determine whether `/tools/json-formatter`, `/blog/how-to-format-json-in-javascript`, `/tools/base64-converter`, and `/blog/what-is-base64-encoding-with-examples` have impressions, positions 10–60, or low CTR. These are provisional P0 candidates because they pair distinct intent with working tools and existing guides. The homepage and `/tools` are P1 navigation pages. Other tool pages are P2 until demand is confirmed. Generic “best tools” and annual roundup posts are P3 and should be consolidated or substantially differentiated if Search Console and user data do not show value.

## Implemented in this pass

Implemented: truthful tool history disclosures; removal of unsupported author/review statements and keyword-facing blog copy; improved 404 navigation and blog breadcrumbs; removed non-functional SearchAction/schema identity; corrected sitemap dates; revised JWT limitations and privacy copy; removed ad slots from tool/article detail pages; homepage now directs users to dedicated tool routes. Lint, TypeScript, and production build pass. Search Console query/page exports, a real author identity and credentials, contact form provider/data retention, legal text review, and an actual content review/testing cadence still require publisher input.
