India GST Invoice Generator

Use this India GST invoice generator to prepare a draft with GSTIN fields, HSN/SAC codes, place of supply and a choice between intra-state and inter-state tax presentation. It runs in your browser; the figures and draft stay on this device unless you choose to download or print them.

A practical GST invoice identifies the supplier, its GSTIN, the recipient where relevant, a consecutive invoice number and the invoice date. Include a clear description, quantity, taxable value and applicable rate for every item. The place of supply matters: it helps determine whether the example should display CGST and SGST or IGST. This tool shows a soft GSTIN format warning, not a government verification.

For an intra-state example, a taxable value of ₹1,000 at 18% produces ₹180 total GST, shown as ₹90 CGST and ₹90 SGST. For an inter-state supply at the same rate, the example shows ₹180 IGST instead. This presentation is arithmetic support only; exemptions, special territories, registration status and supply-specific rules can change the correct treatment.

Enter HSN/SAC codes where relevant, describe the supply clearly, and check the invoice sequence, dates, customer details and place of supply before issuing. Confirm current invoicing rules and any required e-invoice or e-way bill process with the official tax authority or an accountant.

What to include on a India invoice

Show the supplier and recipient details, GSTINs where applicable, invoice number and date, place of supply, HSN/SAC, taxable value, tax rate and tax amount.

  • Supplier name, address and GSTIN
  • Unique invoice number and issue date
  • Recipient details and GSTIN where applicable
  • HSN/SAC, quantity, taxable value and GST rate
  • Place of supply and CGST/SGST or IGST amounts

Worked example

Worked example: three units priced at ₹100 each have a taxable value of ₹300. At 18%, line GST is ₹54. For an intra-state supply, the preview shows ₹27 CGST and ₹27 SGST; for an inter-state supply, it shows ₹54 IGST. A second item at 5% is calculated and grouped separately.

Common invoice mistakes to avoid

  • Leaving the place of supply inconsistent with the transaction
  • Using an HSN/SAC or rate without checking the current classification
  • Treating a format warning as official GSTIN verification
  • Forgetting that a tax invoice may need additional details for the transaction

Frequently asked questions

Does this tool verify a GSTIN?

No. It applies a basic format warning in your browser; it does not contact or query a government service.

When should I select IGST?

Use the inter-state presentation only when your transaction's place-of-supply treatment supports it; confirm the applicable rules.

Can I add HSN or SAC?

Yes. Each line has an optional HSN/SAC field that appears in the preview.

How is tax rounded?

Each line's tax is rounded half-up to the nearest paise before the totals are summed.

Where is my draft saved?

Only in this browser's local storage, unless you explicitly export or print it.

Is this an e-invoice filing service?

No. It creates a printable draft and does not submit an invoice or return.
