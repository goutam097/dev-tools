What to include on a Singapore invoice

A Singapore tax invoice for a standard-rated supply generally includes the supplier's name, address and GST number, invoice date and number, customer details, description, amounts and GST.

  • Supplier name, address and GST registration number
  • Tax Invoice title, date and identifying number
  • Customer name and address
  • Description, quantity and value of each supply
  • GST rate, amount and total payable

Worked example

Worked example: two standard-rated items worth SGD 50 each have SGD 100 net value. At 9%, GST is SGD 9, producing a SGD 109 total. If the prices already include GST, enter the gross prices and select inclusive pricing to extract the tax instead of adding it again.

Common invoice mistakes to avoid

  • Charging GST without confirming GST registration and supply status
  • Using an outdated rate
  • Double-counting GST in inclusive prices
  • Treating an exempt supply as zero-rated

Frequently asked questions

Can I edit Singapore's default GST rate?

Yes. The country rate list is editable in countries.js; verify the current rate before updating.

Does the tool check a GST number with IRAS?

No. It only performs a soft local format check.

Can an invoice use zero-rated supplies?

A 0% line is available, but confirm the supply classification and required records.

Does the inclusive toggle extract GST?

Yes. It calculates the tax portion from an inclusive line price.

Is this connected to IRAS?

No. The static tool makes no network requests and submits no returns.

Can I download my draft?

Yes. Print to PDF or download a JSON backup from the toolbar.
