What to include on a Custom tax invoice

Invoice requirements differ by country and transaction. Adapt the tax label, rate and wording to your local requirements before issuing an invoice.

  • Seller and customer names and addresses
  • Unique invoice number and issue date
  • Description, quantity and price for each item
  • Tax rate and amount where applicable
  • Total payable, currency and payment terms

Worked example

Worked example: a quantity of two at 50 units each gives a 100-unit taxable base. At a custom 10% rate, tax is 10 and the total is 110. Choose the correct currency and local tax wording, then independently verify the required invoice fields.

Common invoice mistakes to avoid

  • Assuming a generic rate or currency format matches local rules
  • Leaving out a required registration number or supply date
  • Using tax-inclusive values while expecting tax to be added
  • Treating a formatted tax ID as verified
