What to include on a United Arab Emirates invoice

A UAE tax invoice generally identifies the supplier and TRN, invoice and supply dates, customer details, goods or services, taxable amounts, VAT rate and VAT charged.

  • Supplier name, address and 15-digit TRN
  • Tax invoice title, unique number and dates
  • Customer name and address where required
  • Description, quantity and taxable value
  • VAT rate, VAT amount and total payable

Worked example

Worked example: two services of AED 400 each total AED 800 before tax. At 5%, VAT is AED 40 and the total is AED 840. For a qualifying zero-rated supply, the invoice still needs the applicable classification and supporting records even though VAT charged is zero.

Common invoice mistakes to avoid

  • Confusing a zero rate with an exempt supply
  • Omitting the TRN or supply date where required
  • Assuming a 15-digit format check confirms that a TRN is active
  • Using reverse-charge wording without checking the relevant supply
