A VAT invoice commonly identifies the supplier and its VAT registration number, a unique sequential invoice number, invoice date and tax point. It also describes the goods or services, quantities, net amounts and VAT charged. Customer details and any required special wording depend on the kind of transaction and whether a full, simplified or modified invoice is appropriate.

For example, a £100 net standard-rated item at 20% adds £20 VAT, making £120 due. A qualifying reduced-rated item at 5% adds £5 per £100 net, while a zero-rated item has no VAT charged. With tax-inclusive entry, the displayed price already contains VAT and the tool extracts the tax portion; always confirm which rate applies to the actual supply.

Rate choices here are editable software defaults, not a ruling about a product's VAT liability; confirm the latest HMRC guidance for unusual, exempt or cross-border supplies.

What to include on a United Kingdom invoice

A VAT invoice generally includes the supplier's VAT number, a unique sequential number, dates, customer details, a description of goods or services, and VAT amounts.

  • Supplier name, address and VAT registration number
  • Unique invoice number and tax point / supply date
  • Customer name and address
  • Description, quantities and net amounts
  • VAT rate and VAT amount for each rate

Worked example

Worked example: £100 net at 20% gives £20 VAT; £100 net at 5% gives £5 VAT; and £100 at 0% gives no VAT. Together the invoice total is £325. If prices include tax, £120 at 20% contains £20 VAT rather than adding another £24.

Common invoice mistakes to avoid

  • Confusing zero-rated supplies with exempt supplies
  • Using the invoice date instead of the relevant tax point without checking
  • Adding VAT to prices that were already entered as tax-inclusive
  • Applying reverse-charge wording to a domestic supply without checking the rules

Frequently asked questions

Can one invoice contain several VAT rates?

Yes. Choose a rate on each line; the preview groups and totals tax by rate.

Does the VAT number get checked with HMRC?

No. The format warning is local and does not verify registration.

Can prices include VAT?

Yes. Use the Prices include tax switch before entering or reviewing gross prices.

Does the tool decide whether a supply is zero-rated?

No. Confirm the correct VAT liability and any required evidence.

Can I add a supply date?

Yes. The supply-date field is included in the invoice details.

Does this send information to HMRC?

No. The generator is client-side and makes no service or API requests.
